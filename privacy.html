Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2025

Auckland Precision Plumbing Ltd ("we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting your personal information. This policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information in accordance with the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020.

1. What information we collect

We collect personal information only when you provide it to us directly. This includes:

  • Contact form submissions: name, phone number, email address, service type, and message content submitted via our contact form.
  • Phone enquiries: name and contact details provided during a phone call.
  • Analytics data: anonymised browser and device information collected automatically via Google Analytics 4 (see section 4).

We do not collect sensitive information (as defined by the Privacy Act 2020) and we do not collect information from individuals under 18 years of age.

2. How we use your information

Information you provide is used solely to:

  • Respond to your service enquiry or request for a quote.
  • Schedule and complete plumbing work you have requested.
  • Send invoices and manage payment for work completed.
  • Communicate important updates about work in progress.

We do not use your information for unsolicited marketing. We will not share your information with third parties for their marketing purposes.

3. How we store your information

Contact form submissions are processed and stored by Formspree (a US-based service) and forwarded to our business email. Email records are retained for up to 3 years for business record purposes, then deleted. Formspree's privacy policy is available at formspree.io/legal/privacy-policy.

We take reasonable steps to protect your information from unauthorised access, loss, or disclosure. However, no internet transmission is completely secure.

4. Google Analytics

This website uses Google Analytics 4 to understand how visitors use our site. Google Analytics collects anonymised data including:

  • Pages visited and time spent on each page.
  • Browser type, device type, and approximate geographic location (country/city level only).
  • Interactions with buttons and links (e.g. quote requests, phone clicks).

This data is anonymised and does not identify you personally. It is processed by Google LLC under their privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy. You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking by installing the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

5. Cookies

This website uses cookies set by Google Analytics to distinguish unique visitors and track sessions. These cookies do not contain personally identifiable information. You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings; note that disabling cookies may affect site functionality.

6. Disclosure to third parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We may disclose your information only:

  • To service providers who assist us in operating our business (e.g. Formspree for form processing, Google for analytics) — only to the extent necessary and subject to appropriate agreements.
  • Where required by New Zealand law or a court order.
  • To protect the safety of any person where there is a serious and credible threat.

7. Your rights under the Privacy Act 2020

Under the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020, you have the right to:

  • Access personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction of any personal information that is incorrect or out of date.
  • Request deletion of personal information we no longer have a lawful basis to retain.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at the details below. We will respond within 20 working days.

8. Contact us about this policy

If you have questions about this privacy policy or wish to exercise your rights, contact us:

  • Email: hello@aucklandprecisionplumbing.co.nz
  • Phone: (09) 555 0123
  • Post: Auckland Precision Plumbing Ltd, Auckland, New Zealand

If you are not satisfied with our response, you may contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects when the policy was last revised. Continued use of this website constitutes acceptance of the current policy.
